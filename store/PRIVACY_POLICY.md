# Privacy Policy — ULY Künupka

**Last updated:** 2026-04-09  
**Product:** ULY Künupka (iOS app + keyboard extension)  
**Contact:** replace-with-your-email@example.com

This policy describes how ULY Künupka handles information. Host this page at a public HTTPS URL and paste that URL into App Store Connect → App Privacy / Privacy Policy URL.

## Summary

- Typing happens **on your device**.
- The keyboard is configured with **Full Access off** by default (`RequestsOpenAccess = false`).
- We do **not** require an account.
- We do **not** include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs in the current product.
- A dictionary for suggestions/spell check is **bundled in the app** and used offline.

## Data we do not collect

The app and keyboard do **not** collect, transmit, or sell:

- Keystrokes or message contents  
- Contacts, photos, location, or identifiers for advertising  
- Analytics events to our servers  
- Crash logs to a third-party service (unless you later add one and update this policy)

## Data stored on your device

The keyboard may store limited preferences and learned words **locally** on your iPhone, for example:

- Theme preference (System / Light / Dark)  
- Words you accept from suggestions (to improve local suggestions)

This data stays in the keyboard’s sandbox and is not uploaded by the app.

## Internet access

Typing features work offline. The app does not need network access for suggestions or spell check. If a future version adds optional online features, this policy will be updated before release.

## Children’s privacy

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not target children with data collection. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices

- Remove the keyboard in **Settings → General → Keyboard** to stop using it.  
- Delete the app to remove the host app and its local data.  
- Reinstalling clears local keyboard preferences unless iOS restores them from backup.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date will change when we do. Material changes that affect App Store privacy answers will be reflected in App Store Connect as well.
